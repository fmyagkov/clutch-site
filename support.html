Support

Clutch is a daily mental training app for athletes: short, measured drills for reaction, control, decision-making, awareness, focus, memory, timing and composure.

For help, questions or feedback, email fedor@oterahealth.com. We aim to reply within two working days.

Common questions

Where is my data?

On your phone only. Clutch has no accounts and does not send your results anywhere. See the privacy policy.

How do I start over?

Delete Clutch and install it again. This erases all saved results, and it cannot be undone.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Clutch has no sync of its own. If you restore your new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, your Clutch data should come with it.

Why are my reaction times different on another device?

Every screen adds its own small delay. Reaction times are meant for tracking yourself on one device over time, not for comparing between devices or people.

How do levels work?

Each drill has thirty levels. Your first run places you. After that, a score of 95 or more moves you up three levels, 90 or more moves you up two, two scores of 80 to 89 move you up one, and a score under 50 moves you down one. You can replay any earlier level for practice from a drill's opening screen.

What is the clutch rep?

The last step of each daily session: one attempt, with no retries, to match your weakest drill score of that session.

I can't hear the sound cues

Check that "Sound cues" is on at the bottom of the home screen and that your phone's volume is up.

Is Clutch a medical or psychological assessment?

No. It is a training and self-reflection tool. The mindset check is not a validated clinical test.
