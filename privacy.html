Clutch

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Clutch: Mental Training ("Clutch") is published by Otera Health. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Clutch does not collect it.

What we collect

Nothing. Clutch has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else. The app does not connect to our servers at all.

What stays on your device

Clutch saves the following on your phone so it can show your progress:

  • the sport you choose;
  • your drill results, levels, streak and session history;
  • your daily check-in answers and mindset check results;
  • your sound setting.

This information never leaves your device through Clutch. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Tracking, advertising and analytics

Clutch contains no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools, and it does not share data with third parties.

Permissions

Clutch does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, health records or notifications.

Backups

If you back up your phone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include Clutch's saved data in that backup. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Information from Apple

Apple may give app developers summary statistics, such as download counts and crash reports, from people who have agreed to share them. These do not identify you.

Deleting your data

Deleting Clutch from your phone deletes everything it has saved.

Children

Clutch is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Clutch is a training and self-reflection tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and its mindset check is not a clinical assessment.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of Clutch begins to collect any information, we will say so here before it does.
